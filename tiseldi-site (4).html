Our Story

Every fiber starts from the earth,
and comes back to live in your home.

The Tiseldi cooperative brings together artisans from the Ouarzazate region around a shared gesture: weaving local plant fibers — palm, reed, raffia — into lasting objects of light and décor.

Every piece begins with raw material harvested by hand, sun-dried, then woven using techniques passed from mother to daughter. No factory, no two pieces alike: the weave always keeps the trace of the hand that made it.

Our Craft

From raw fiber to finished piece

01

Harvest

Raffia, reed and palm are harvested locally, then sorted fiber by fiber for flexibility and color.

02

Drying

The fibers dry in the Ouarzazate sun, which gives them the golden hue characteristic of our pieces.

03

Weaving

Every shape — dome, arch, fringe — comes to life in the artisans' hands, with no mold or machine.

04

Finishing

Structure, wiring and details are fitted to each piece, then checked one by one before shipping.
